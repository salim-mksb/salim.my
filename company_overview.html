Our Journey

How We Started

Established in 1996, Melawati Kebab Sdn. Bhd. began its journey in the frozen food industry, specializing in premium chicken and meat-based products.

Co-founded by Mr. Mohd Shafiee bin Salim and Mrs. Zawiyah binti Basiran, the enterprise originated as a modest street vendor before expanding into a modern manufacturing facility.

In response to rapidly growing demand, they expanded operations to deliver high-quality, Halal-certified products tailored to customer preferences.

Today, the Salim brand stands as one of Malaysia's most recognized and trusted names in the frozen food sector.
