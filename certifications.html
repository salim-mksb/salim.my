STEP-BY-STEP CONTROL

OUR 4-STAGE QUALITY WORKFLOW

1

Certified Raw Meat Inspection

Stage 01

Raw meat cutlets arrive under strict temperature-controlled logistics from accredited Halal abattoirs. Immediate inspection ensures ideal freshness, color, and cold preservation.

2

Automated Hygienic Manufacturing

Stage 02

Inside cleanroom facilities with air filtration systems, staff in full protective suits operate automated marinating, blending, and kebab cone-shaping equipment.

3

Rapid Industrial Flash Freezing

Stage 03

Products undergo blast freezing down to -18°C instantly. This locks in authentic taste, tender texture, and natural moisture without needing artificial chemicals.

4

Lab Sampling & Barcode Tracking

Stage 04

Every finished lot undergoes metal detector screening, microbial lab testing, and barcode assignment for end-to-end batch traceability before store delivery.
