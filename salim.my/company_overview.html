About Melawati Kebab Sdn Bhd

Company Overview

Pioneering premium Halal frozen food manufacturing, authentic kebab production, and trusted food distribution across Malaysia for over two decades.

Who We Are

A Legacy of Quality, Taste & Authenticity

Established in 1996, Melawati Kebab Sdn Bhd (778900-M) has grown into a premier manufacturer and supplier of high-quality Halal frozen food products in Malaysia. Headquartered in Taman Desa Cheras, Kuala Lumpur, we specialise in delicious, convenient, and ready-to-prepare meals crafted for both retail consumers and B2B business partners.

With over 20 years of industry experience, our dedication to uncompromising hygiene, superior ingredient selection, and strict Halalan Toyyiban compliance has earned the trust of families, restaurants, and distributors nationwide.

  • 20+ Years of Frozen Food Manufacturing Excellence
  • JAKIM Halal & MeSTI (KKM) Fully Certified Facility
  • Recognized "Made In Malaysia" Quality Brand
