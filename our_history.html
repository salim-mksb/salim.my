Our Heritage

Our Journey, Our Legacy

Founded in 1996 as Melawati Kebab, we have grown from our humble beginnings into a trusted frozen food manufacturer, continuing to deliver quality and delicious products to this day.

The Leadership

Mohd Shafiee bin Salim & Zawiyah binti Basiran

Director and Co-Director of Melawati Kebab Sdn Bhd

Established with a shared passion for delivering authentic, high-quality, and 100% Halal culinary products to Malaysian families and businesses.

"Our mission from day one has been to preserve authentic taste, uncompromising cleanliness, and genuine Halal integrity in every meal we manufacture."

Establishment of Melawati Kebab
